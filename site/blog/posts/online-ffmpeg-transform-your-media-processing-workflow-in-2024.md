## What Is Online FFmpeg and Why Do You Need It?

In today’s digital landscape, **online FFmpeg** tools have become indispensable for video and audio processing. With the rise of cloud-based solutions, **online FFmpeg** platforms now allow users to execute complex commands without installing software. This article explores how these tools simplify workflows and why they are essential for creators, developers, and businesses.

- **Format Conversion:** Convert videos between MP4, MOV, AVI, and more.
